Done.

1. Plug in and switch on the printer (USB).
2. Open System Settings > Printers & Scanners. The printer appears as "ORGSTA T001Plus" (if a queue was not already created for you).
3. Print. The print dialog pre-selects your system paper size (A4/Letter): choose 100 x 150 mm once, then Presets > Save Current Settings as Preset (only this printer). macOS re-applies it automatically from then on.
4. If the LED blinks red after the first label, hold the FEED button 3-5 seconds to calibrate the gap sensor.

Uninstall: sudo /Library/Printers/OpenTSPL/uninstall.sh
